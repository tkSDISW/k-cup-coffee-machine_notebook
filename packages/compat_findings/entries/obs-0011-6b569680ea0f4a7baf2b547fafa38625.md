## Compat finding 11: setting the `prefix` of requirements by patch (confirmation, local commits), and how the engineer's Capella links read back

**Result:** worked as expected. No problems found. **Pending:** the engineer's check in Capella 6.1 after push that the prefix shows in the UI.

1. **Tool and calls**
   - `pull_model_changes` (one incoming commit, `3ba01ef` "Update", by the engineer in Capella 6.1), then `browse_model(OA, Requirement)` to re-browse before patching.
   - `apply_model_patch` with `parent: !uuid <requirement>` and `set: prefix: "UR-B-04"` on one requirement (a test), then one patch with 15 such entries for the other requirements.
   - Read-back: `resolve_model_uuids` on all 16 requirements and `generate_fabric`.
2. **Context:** Capella 6.1 model `K Cup Coffee Maker.aird`. Model before: `3ba01ef`. New local commits: `fa06d27f` (UR-B-04 test) and `e99e2a53` (15 requirements). **Neither is pushed.** The request came from the engineer: the prefix is used in the Capella UI, so it should carry the requirement ID that is already the requirement's name.
3. **Expected vs actual**
   - **Set by `set:` on `prefix`:** both patches returned status `ok` and were committed.
   - **Read-back:** `resolve_model_uuids` now includes a `prefix` field for requirements, and all 16 prefixes equal their names (UR-B-01 to UR-B-04 and UR-G-01 to UR-G-12). `generate_fabric` shows the same values in its `prefix` field. Before the patches, the prefix was empty for all 16 (Compat findings 3 and 4).
4. **Observation, prefix can be written and read:** unlike a requirement's `description` (Compat finding 4), the `prefix` is visible in the tool read-back, so the written value can be confirmed from the tools.
5. **Observation, the engineer's links so far** (from the same fabric read-back; a status check, not a test of the tools): two requirement links drawn in Capella are present, both named "Satisfy" and of type `CapellaOutgoingRelation`: UR-B-04 to "Prepare a serving of hot coffee on demand" (unchanged), and UR-B-01 to "Obtain ready-to-use coffee supplies in variety" (new relation `63fb289e-5eaf-42de-828d-cce2c33073b4`). The reversed patch-made link on UR-B-01 (`0e4e70c4-e630-4810-9b18-996ddbcf869c`) is gone. The read-back lists the requirement as `source` and the capability as `target`, as in Compat finding 9.
6. **Reproduction:** open a session at `3ba01ef` and apply the `set: prefix:` patch against any requirement UUID, then `resolve_model_uuids` and `generate_fabric`.
7. **Scope:** none observed; nothing points specifically to 6.1.
8. **Workaround:** not needed.
9. **Check in Capella 6.1:** **pending** after push. To check: each requirement shows its ID as prefix in the UI, the model opens normally, and the two links are intact.
10. **Tool version or build:** not known (open item in DEC-0007).

**Not yet covered:** setting the other requirement fields by patch (chapter name, requirement type); `description` read-back for requirements.
