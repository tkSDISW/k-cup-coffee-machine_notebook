## Compat finding 15: `exchange_items:` inside the `exchange:` creation form is accepted but silently ignored

**Result:** the patch returned `ok` and committed, but **no exchange item was allocated**. There was no error and no warning. A second patch was needed.

1. **Tool and call:** `apply_model_patch` with 29 `exchange:` entries, each with `name`, `description`, `source`, `target` and an additional key:
   ```yaml
   - exchange:
       name: Coffee supplies
       description: "..."
       source: !uuid <source-activity>
       target: !uuid <target-activity>
       exchange_items:
         - !uuid 34815d9c-1ce5-4f34-84ee-7e97a0717b40
   ```
2. **Context:** Capella 6.1 model `K Cup Coffee Maker.aird`. Local commit `b263c841` (the 29 interactions), then `65bcea54` (the fix).
3. **Expected vs actual**
   - Expected: each interaction created with its exchange item allocated.
   - Actual: status `ok`, committed. `generate_fabric` showed the 29 interactions with source and target, but **no `allocated exchange items`** on any of them, and only the first interaction (allocated by a separate patch earlier) showed one. The 12 exchange items not referenced anywhere were absent from the read-back.
4. **Workaround that works:** create the interaction first, then allocate with `parent: !uuid <interaction>` and `extend: exchange_items:` holding `!uuid <item>`; this was confirmed for all 30 interactions in the read-back.
5. **Why it matters:** because the patch reports success, the missing allocation is only found by reading back. An unknown key in the `exchange:` form could return an error, or the form could support `exchange_items:`.
6. **Reproduction:** create one interaction with `exchange_items:` in the form, then `generate_fabric` with its source activity resolved, and look for `allocated exchange items`.
7. **Scope:** unknown; likely how the `exchange:` form handles extra keys. Nothing points to 6.1 specifically.
8. **Check in Capella 6.1:** **pending** after push (the exchange items shown on the interactions).
9. **Tool version or build:** not known (open item in DEC-0007).

**Suggestion to the tool maintainer:** reject unknown keys in the `exchange:` form with a clear message, or support `exchange_items:` there.
