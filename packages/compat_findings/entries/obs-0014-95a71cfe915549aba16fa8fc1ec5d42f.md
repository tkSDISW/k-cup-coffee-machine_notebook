## Compat finding 14: operational activities, allocation to entities, exchange items and OA interactions by patch (confirmation; the Handbook note says interactions cannot be created)

**Result:** worked as expected. No problems found. **Pending:** the engineer's check in Capella 6.1 after push. One documentation mismatch to resolve.

1. **Tool and calls** (all `apply_model_patch`)
   - **Activities:** `parent: !uuid <Root Operational Activity>` with `extend: activities:` and 28 entries (`name`, `description`). `_type` was not given.
   - **Allocation to entities:** `parent: !uuid <entity>` with `extend: activities:` holding `!uuid <activity>` references (13 for one entity, then 15 across five entities).
   - **Exchange items:** `parent: !uuid <OA Data package>` with `extend: exchange_items:` and 13 entries.
   - **Interactions:** the `exchange:` form with no parent: `exchange: {name, description, source: !uuid <activity>, target: !uuid <activity>}`. One interaction first as a test, then 29 in one patch.
   - **Allocating an exchange item onto an interaction:** `parent: !uuid <interaction>` with `extend: exchange_items:` holding `!uuid <item>`.
   - Read-back with `browse_model`, `resolve_model_uuids`, `generate_fabric`, and `verify_model(phase="OA")`.
2. **Context:** Capella 6.1 model `K Cup Coffee Maker.aird`. Model at the start: `cb4e327`. New local commits, **none pushed**: `e98c910e` (28 activities), `fdc4acfa` and `f9fd1065` (allocations), `c52a90cb` (13 exchange items), `7a182de4` (first interaction), `8f3df267` (first item allocation), `b263c841` (29 interactions), `65bcea54` (29 item allocations).
3. **Expected vs actual**
   - **Activities:** all 28 created as `OperationalActivity` with parent "Root Operational Activity".
   - **Allocation:** each entity's `allocated activities` lists its activities in the read-back (13, 8, 2, 1, 2 and 2).
   - **Exchange items:** 13 `ExchangeItem` elements with parent "Data".
   - **Interactions:** 30 `FunctionalExchange` elements. The read-back shows each one's source and target activity, the activity's `inputs to` and `outputs from`, and, once allocated, `allocated exchange items`.
   - `verify_model(OA)`: no findings (0 issues) after the whole set.
4. **Documentation mismatch:** the Handbook `mcp_tools` note "capella-fabric: What It Won't Do, and Why" (dated 2026-09-15) says functional exchanges cannot be created by patch because they connect ports. The tool's current description says OA interactions between activities can be created with the `exchange:` form, and that is what worked here (no ports were involved at the OA level). The Handbook note may be out of date or may refer to exchanges outside OA; worth updating or clarifying.
5. **Observation:** the interaction's owning activity is chosen by the tool (the endpoints' lowest common ancestor); here every interaction is owned by the Root Operational Activity.
6. **Reproduction:** open a session at `cb4e327` and apply the patches above in order.
7. **Scope:** none observed; nothing points specifically to 6.1.
8. **Workaround:** not needed.
