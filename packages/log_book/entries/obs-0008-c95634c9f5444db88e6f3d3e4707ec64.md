## Review: first operational entities and the OCB diagram

**What was reviewed.** Model commit `d3f9a29` ("Created OCB", committed 2026-11-23, 11:48 US Eastern), pulled into the agent session. It adds the diagram **[OCB] Operational Capabilities** and four operational entities, and links them to the 12 capabilities. The diagram's visual content was not read (the tools list the diagram but do not show its layout); the review is of the model elements and their links.

### What works well
- **The cast matches the draft.** The four entities correspond to the drinker, the equipment, the supply side and support in NOTE-0002.
- **Involvement is sensible.** Supply is tied to capability 3 and support to capability 12. Capability 8 (return to readiness after idle) involves only the equipment, which fits its being equipment behavior and supports the earlier note that it may move to System Analysis.
- **The equipment is an operational entity.** This answers tuning question 3 in NOTE-0002 (equipment as an entity) in favor of including it, and DEC-0008 says its name should then be generic.
- **The capability to entity links exist in the model,** not only in the diagram, so they can be read and checked by tools and reused downstream.

### Comments for tuning
1. **Spelling:** "Coffer Machine" looks like a typo for "Coffee".
2. **Names against DEC-0008 and the behavior config:** "K-Cup Supply Chain" and "K Cup Machine Support" carry a product term into entity names, and the hyphenation differs between them. Neutral candidates: *Coffee Supply Chain* (or *Coffee Supplier*), *Equipment Support* (or *Manufacturer Support*), *Coffee-Making Equipment* (or *Coffee Maker*), and *Coffee Drinker* for the actor, since the actor's role is drinking coffee, not operating a machine. The product name can stay in descriptions and source citations.
3. **Entities the draft listed that are not yet in the model:** household waste handling, a water source and a power supply.
   - **Waste handling matters most.** Capability 4 and the sustainability goals (UR-G-01 to UR-G-04) are about what is discarded, and under "coffee in, coffee out" that waste has to go somewhere. Without a waste-handling entity, there is nothing to receive used coffee remains and packaging.
   - **Water and power** are needed for UR-G-05 and UR-G-06 (energy and water per serving) and for capabilities 6 and 7. One combined "utilities" entity is an option.
   - Retailer or delivery was folded into the supply chain, consistent with a simple first pass (tuning question 5).
4. **Actor flags:** only the user is an actor, so the supply chain, support and equipment are modeled inside the organization under study. That is a legitimate choice. Confirm it is intended, since it affects what becomes an actor when the system boundary is drawn in System Analysis.
5. **Descriptions are empty:** short neutral descriptions (role and what each entity does) would help reviewers and tools. Allocated activities come next.
6. **Open links:** requirements are still not linked to capabilities, and the OA has no activities, scenarios or interactions yet.

### Against the paper's checklist (DEC-0010), first pass
- **Need side kept apart from solution:** yes, nothing at this level names a design element except the two entity names noted in comment 2.
- **Completeness:** every capability involves at least one entity, but none has a scenario or functional chain yet. That is expected at this stage.
