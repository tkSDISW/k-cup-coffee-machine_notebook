## DRAFT: base user requirements (what the user needs to see from use)

**Status: working draft for tuning; nothing is in the model yet.** These are requirements as seen from use, written in solution-agnostic terms (DEC-0008): they do not mention the pod or other design elements. "The system" means the coffee-making system as a whole. Companion entry: goal (reach) requirements. Capability numbers refer to NOTE-0002 (revision 2). Evidence tags: **U** stated by the user, **S** sourced, **I** inferred (to confirm), **A** assumed. Values shown as **TBD** are to be set during tuning.

**Motivating use case (generalized):** a household whose members prefer different coffees, plus a lighter option for later in the day.

### Base requirements
| ID | Requirement | Rationale | Capability | Verification | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UR-B-01 | The system shall support at least three distinct coffee varieties: one preferred by a first drinker, one preferred by a second drinker, and one reduced-caffeine variety (for example half-caffeine) for daytime use. | Different preferences in one household; a lighter option for later in the day. | 2, 3 | Demonstration serving each of three varieties; confirm the reduced-caffeine variety is distinguishable from the others. | U |
| UR-B-02 | The system shall provide the coffee at a serving temperature of no less than **Tmin** and no more than **Tmax** at the point of delivery. Values: **TBD**. The measurement shall be defined: vessel, probe position, and time after delivery starts. | Coffee should be hot enough to enjoy and not hotter than a stated upper limit. | 1 | Temperature measurement under the defined conditions, repeated across varieties and amounts. | U (limits needed); values TBD |
| UR-B-03 | Coffee strength shall be defined against a stated **baseline**, and every variety and strength setting shall be specified relative to that baseline. The baseline measure and the measurement method shall be recorded. | Strength can be compared across varieties and designs only if it is defined the same way. | 2 | Strength measured by the chosen method (for example a refractometer reading) and compared with the baseline. | U |
| UR-B-04 | The coffee preparation cycle time shall be defined and bounded: **(a)** from request to coffee available at serving temperature when the system is ready (**TBD**); **(b)** from a cold or idle start (**TBD**); **(c)** between consecutive servings (**TBD**). | Convenience depends on how long a cup takes. | 1, 6, 8 | Timed runs under each starting condition, using the same definitions of start and finish. | U (cycle time needed); values TBD |

### Baselines and reference data points (evidence, not requirements)
- **Strength baseline, candidate:** the brewing control chart associated with the Specialty Coffee Association's "Golden Cup" standard, which places brew strength at about 1.15 to 1.35 percent total dissolved solids (11.5 to 13.5 g/L) with an extraction yield of about 18 to 22 percent. Source: [SCA Coffee Standards (PDF)](https://sca.coffee/s/Coffee-Standards-1.pdf), also described in [Batali, Ristenpart and Guinard, Scientific Reports, 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-73341-4). That paper also notes the chart is debated on sensory-methodology grounds and that consumer preferences cover a wide range, so the baseline remains a choice for tuning, and the system may define its own named baseline. Evidence: **S**.
- **Brew water temperature, for reference:** the same paper reports that the SCA handbook calls for water at 92 to 96 °C for proper extraction. Evidence: **S**. (This differs from the serving temperature in UR-B-02; both may be needed.)
- **Published figures for the K-Duo, for reference:** brew temperature 170 to 190 °F (iFixit page, OBS-0002); about 4 minutes initial heating; about 1 minute reheat between brews; sizes 6, 8, 10 and 12 oz (K-Duo guide, OBS-0006). Evidence: **S**. Listed as data points only.

### Proposed additions for tuning (from the operational indicators in DEC-0005; confirm before adopting)
- **Hands-on steps per serving** at or below **TBD**, and **clean-up actions per serving** at or below **TBD**, each compared against a defined traditional-preparation baseline. Evidence: **I**.
- **Switching varieties** between consecutive servings without extra cleaning or set-up beyond **TBD**. Evidence: **I**, from UR-B-01.

### Questions for tuning
1. **Temperature:** does UR-B-02 mean the temperature the drinker experiences (as drafted), the brew water temperature, or both?
2. **Strength baseline:** adopt the control-chart measure, define our own named baseline, or both?
3. **Cycle time:** which of (a), (b) and (c) matter most, and what values would feel acceptable?
4. **Varieties:** is "distinct" about flavor and roast, caffeine level, or both? Should a minimum number per day or per week be part of the requirement?

Wording is descriptive and neutral. Values are placeholders for tuning, not statements about any existing product.
