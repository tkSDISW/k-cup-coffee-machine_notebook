## DRAFT (revision 2): base user requirements (what the user needs to see from use)

**Status: working draft for tuning; nothing is in the model yet.** Revision 2 applies the tuning choices in DEC-0009. Requirements are written as seen from use, in solution-agnostic terms (DEC-0008): they do not mention the pod or other design elements. "The system" means the coffee-making system as a whole. Companion entry: goal (reach) requirements (NOTE-0004). Capability numbers refer to NOTE-0002 (revision 2). Evidence tags: **U** stated by the user, **S** sourced, **I** inferred (to confirm), **A** assumed. Values shown as **TBD** are to be set during tuning.

**Motivating use case (generalized):** a household whose members prefer different coffees, plus a lighter option for later in the day.

### Baselines and reference data points (evidence, not requirements)
- **Baseline (a), candidate:** the brewing control chart associated with the Specialty Coffee Association's "Golden Cup" standard, which places brew strength at about 1.15 to 1.35 percent total dissolved solids (11.5 to 13.5 g/L) with an extraction yield of about 18 to 22 percent. Source: [SCA Coffee Standards (PDF)](https://sca.coffee/s/Coffee-Standards-1.pdf), also described in [Batali, Ristenpart and Guinard, Scientific Reports, 2020](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-73341-4). That paper notes the chart is debated on sensory-methodology grounds and that consumer preferences cover a wide range, which is one reason a second, project-defined baseline is useful. Evidence: **S**.
- **Baseline (b), to define:** a named project baseline, for example a reference brew defined by amount, strength measure and method, to be agreed. Evidence: **A**.
- **Brew water temperature, for reference only:** the same paper reports that the SCA handbook calls for water at 92 to 96 °C for proper extraction. This is a derived value for later phases, not a user requirement (DEC-0009). Evidence: **S**.
- **Published figures for the K-Duo, for reference:** brew temperature 170 to 190 °F (iFixit page, OBS-0002); about 4 minutes initial heating; about 1 minute reheat between brews; sizes 6, 8, 10 and 12 oz (K-Duo guide, OBS-0006). Evidence: **S**. Listed as data points only.

### Proposed additions for tuning (from the operational indicators in DEC-0005; confirm before adopting)
- **Hands-on steps per serving** at or below **TBD**, and **clean-up actions per serving** at or below **TBD**, each compared against a defined traditional-preparation baseline. Evidence: **I**.
- **Switching varieties** between consecutive servings without extra cleaning or set-up beyond **TBD**. Evidence: **I**, from UR-B-01.

### Resolved in revision 2
- Temperature is from the drinker's perspective (DEC-0009 item 1).
- Strength uses both a published reference and a project baseline (DEC-0009 item 2).

### Questions still open
1. **Project baseline (b):** what amount, measure and method define it? A reference brew of a stated amount measured with a refractometer is one option.
2. **Cycle time:** which of (a), (b) and (c) matter most, and what values would feel acceptable?
3. **Varieties:** is "distinct" about flavor and roast, caffeine level, or both? Should a minimum number per day or per week be part of the requirement?

Wording is descriptive and neutral. Values are placeholders for tuning, not statements about any existing product.
