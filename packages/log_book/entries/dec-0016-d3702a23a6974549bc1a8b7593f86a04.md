## Decision: operational activities, exchange items and interactions are in the model; the engineer builds the processes

**Decided by the engineer** ("patch the changes"), after the draft in NOTE-0007. The draft was patched in as written; the tuning questions in that note were not answered, so the content can still be tuned.

**Division of work (as agreed):** the agent creates the activities and exchanges and allocates them to the entities; **the engineer creates the operational processes in Capella and maps them to the operational capabilities.**

**What is now in the model** (local commits, not pushed; see Push status)
- **28 operational activities** under the Root Operational Activity, each allocated to one entity: Coffee Drinker 13, Coffee-Making Equipment 8, Coffee Supply Chain 2, Waste Handling 2, Utilities Supply 2, Equipment Support 1. Each description names the capability it supports and points to NOTE-0007.
- **13 exchange items** in the OA Data package: Serving request, Coffee supplies, Water, Electrical power, Prepared coffee, Served coffee, Used coffee remains and packaging, Disposal guidance, Spills and drips, Equipment status, Used cleaning liquid, Support request, Guidance and replacement parts. Several descriptions say which requirement is measured on the item (for example Served coffee for UR-B-02 and UR-B-03, Used coffee remains and packaging for UR-G-01 and UR-G-03).
- **30 operational interactions** between activities, each named for what it carries and each carrying its exchange item. They correspond to the 30 interactions in NOTE-0007 and are grouped there into the candidate processes PR1 to PR8.
- **Not created:** operational processes (the engineer's task), entity-level communication means, scenarios.
- `verify_model(OA)` is clean.

**Candidate processes for the engineer to build** (from NOTE-0007; the interactions that make up each are listed there)
PR1 Prepare a serving of coffee; PR2 Obtain and load coffee supplies; PR3 Dispose of used coffee remains and packaging; PR4 Contain and clear spills and drips; PR5 Keep the equipment supplied with water; PR6 Make the equipment ready and return it to readiness; PR7 Keep the equipment clean and performing; PR8 Store, move, and obtain support. *Store or move the equipment* has no interaction, so it stays outside PR8's chain for now.

**How requirements can attach** (DEC-0010): temperature and strength on Served coffee; waste and diversion on Used coffee remains and packaging; packaging mass on Coffee supplies; energy on Electrical power; water on Water; and the cycle time (UR-B-04) on the process PR1, once the engineer has built it.

**Tooling notes** (details in Compat findings 13 to 15): OA interactions can be created by patch with the `exchange:` form, contrary to the older Handbook note; an exchange item must be allocated to an interaction in a second step, because naming it inside the creation form is silently ignored; and an expired model session needs a fresh clone.
