## Decision: review the requirements against the Bonnet, Voirin and Navas (2019) paper

**Decided by the engineer.** The requirements drafts (NOTE-0003 base, NOTE-0004 goals) and their alignment to the operational capabilities (NOTE-0002) will be **reviewed against the paper**, which is recorded as a reference in the entry "Reference: Augmenting requirements with models" (OBS-0007).

**Why:** the drafts already link each requirement to operational capabilities. The paper offers a tested way to decide which requirements should be expressed as model elements, which should stay textual, and how to link and verify them, which is what the model will need once OA content is added.

**When the review happens**
1. After the engineer finishes tuning the three drafts.
2. Before OA content is added to the model.
3. Again when textual requirements are linked to model elements in System Analysis.

**Review checklist (drawn from the paper)**
1. **Need and solution kept apart.** Each requirement and model element sits on the right side. Solution-driven requirements attach to Logical or Physical Architecture, consistent with DEC-0008.
2. **Form of each requirement:** model requirement (capability, function, exchange, functional chain, scenario, mode or state, data type, class or physical quantity), textual requirement, or both linked.
3. **Links:** justification or traceability links from model to textual requirements; satisfaction links from textual requirements to model elements; scoped to components when a requirement applies to only part of the system.
4. **Wording and verifiability:** statements reformulated into "upon a user action, the system shall..." style where applicable; each requirement carries a verification method (inspection, analysis, demonstration or test) and traceability.
5. **V&V through the model:** capabilities, scenarios and functional chains (nominal and degraded) are the content of the verification cases; a model requirement is verified when its tests pass, a textual requirement when its linked model requirements are verified.
6. **Separation of artifacts:** need-originated requirements apart from solution requirements, in separate modules.
7. **Completeness:** every capability has scenarios or functional chains; every textual requirement links to a model element or is justified as not expressible in the model.

**First look (preliminary, not the formal review)**
| Requirement | Likely form | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| UR-B-01 variety | both | Capabilities and scenarios per variety; a coffee supply exchange item with a variety attribute; text keeps the "at least three" statement. |
| UR-B-02 serving temperature | both | A physical quantity with minimum and maximum on the delivered coffee; text keeps the measurement definition. |
| UR-B-03 strength baselines | both | A strength property on the delivered coffee; text keeps the two baselines and the method. |
| UR-B-04 cycle time | mostly model | A latency constraint on functional chains for ready-state, cold-start and between-servings cases, the paper's own example type. |
| UR-G-01, G-04 waste and packaging mass | both | Exchange items for used coffee remains and packaging with a mass property flowing to waste handling; text keeps the goal values and the baseline rule. |
| UR-G-03 divertible remains | both | A disposal scenario plus a textual goal. |
| UR-G-05, G-06, G-07 energy, water, consumables | both | Exchange items with quantities per serving on functional chains; text keeps the targets. |
| UR-G-02 recyclability or compostability, G-08 life cycle method, G-09 service life and end-of-life | mostly textual | Standards-based criteria and maintenance or life periods are the kind the paper says are hard to express in the model; link by satisfaction to model elements. |
| UR-G-10, G-11, G-12 stretch goals | attributes | Goal values on the same model elements as the base requirements. |

**Gaps to resolve in the review**
- Our **base versus goal** values are not covered by the paper; decide how to represent a threshold and a goal on the same element (for example two properties or a separate requirement linked to it).
- The **verification column** in the drafts uses free text; add the inspection, analysis, demonstration or test category.
- The model has no requirements module yet; decide whether to use Capella's requirements add-on, another module or an external tool, and record the 6.1 compatibility finding if the tools are involved.

**Neutral wording:** the review checks structure and traceability. It is not a judgment of any current product.
