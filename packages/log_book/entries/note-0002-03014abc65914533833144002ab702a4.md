## DRAFT: candidate operational capabilities for the single-serve pod brewer (for tuning before adding to the model)

**Status: working draft, nothing is in the model yet.** Scope is pod-brewing mode only (DEC-0004). The organizing thesis is convenience and lack of mess (DEC-0005). Basis is the K-Duo guide in OBS-0006; evidence tags: **S** sourced from the guide, **I** inferred, **A** assumed. "Drivers": **C** convenience, **M** lack of mess.

Wording is capability-level and system-agnostic, as OA requires: it says what the operational community can do, not how the brewer does it.

### Core
| # | Candidate capability | Need served | Drivers | Likely involved entities | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare a single serving of hot coffee on demand | a cup now, without preparing a whole pot | C | coffee drinker, pod brewer, pod | S: single-cup brew steps |
| 2 | Match the serving to the occasion (size and strength) | different cups for different moods and mugs | C | coffee drinker | S: four sizes (6, 8, 10, 12 oz), STRONG option |
| 3 | Supply sealed single-serve pods in variety | portioned coffee without measuring or handling grounds | C, M | pod producer, retailer or delivery, coffee drinker | S: variety statement; I: sealed pod keeps grounds contained (to confirm in the teardown source) |
| 4 | Dispose of a used pod without handling loose grounds | removal and disposal in one piece | M | coffee drinker, household waste handling | S: remove used pod from holder; I: grounds stay in the pod |
| 5 | Contain and clear overflow and drips | a clean counter and vessel placement | M | coffee drinker, drip tray, vessel | S: drip tray holds up to 8 oz, emptied occasionally; vessel placement and travel-mug option |

### Enabling
| # | Candidate capability | Need served | Drivers | Likely involved entities | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Make the brewer ready for use (first use and after storage) | go from box or storage to a first cup quickly | C | coffee drinker, power supply | S: plug, fill, rinse, cleansing brew, about 4 minutes initial heating |
| 7 | Keep the brewer supplied with water | a ready water supply | C | coffee drinker, water source, optional water filter | S: fill to MAX line, filter option, water quality advice |
| 8 | Return to readiness after idle | save energy without a long wait | C | coffee drinker, power supply | S: auto off after 5 minutes; about 1 minute reheat between brews |
| 9 | Operate in a range of conditions | same simple routine at altitude or with different vessels | C | coffee drinker | S: high altitude setting; vessel options |

### Lifecycle and support

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Keep the brewer clean and performing (routine cleaning and descaling) | consistent cups over time with modest effort | C, M | coffee drinker, descaling supplies | S: needle, pod holder, drip tray and reservoir care; descale every 3 months, about 75 minutes |
| 11 | Operate safely around heat, electricity and sharp parts | safe everyday use | none | coffee drinker, household | S: needle caution, hot liquid |
| 12 | Store, move, and obtain support or replacement parts | the brewer's whole ownership lifetime | C | coffee drinker, manufacturer support | S: storage and transport steps, parts and warranty contact |

### Candidates that may move or be dropped when tuning
- **8 and 9** may read better as System Analysis functions or scenarios than as OA capabilities.
- **11** may be a cross-cutting constraint, not a capability.
- **10 and 12** may be one lifecycle capability or stay split; splitting keeps cleaning, which supports the "no mess" thesis, visible.
- Capabilities **3 and 4** are where the pod's design shows up most in OA, since they move portioning and grounds containment upstream to the pod producer. They should carry the operational comparison with preparing coffee the traditional way (DEC-0005).

### Questions for tuning
1. **Granularity:** about a dozen capabilities, or fewer, higher-level ones with the detail in activities and scenarios?
2. **Entities:** is the pod brewer itself an operational entity in OA, or does it stay outside until System Analysis?
3. **Baseline:** do we add the traditional preparation process as a comparison now, to quantify the convenience and mess indicators (DEC-0005)?
4. **Ecosystem:** do we model pod suppliers beyond one generic pod producer, given the source statements about brands and compatible accessories? (Recorded without adopting a position.)

### Next steps
Tune the list with the engineer; then derive operational entities, activities and scenarios; then add to the model through `capella-fabric` with a small first patch (this will also be Compat finding 2).
