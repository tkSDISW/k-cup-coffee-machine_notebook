## DRAFT (revision 2): candidate operational capabilities for making a cup of coffee (for tuning before adding to the model)

**Status: working draft, nothing is in the model yet.** Revision 2 applies DEC-0008: capabilities are solution-agnostic and do not mention the pod or other design terms. Organizing thesis: convenience and lack of mess (DEC-0005). Evidence is from the K-Duo guide in OBS-0006; tags: **S** sourced, **I** inferred, **A** assumed. Drivers: **C** convenience, **M** lack of mess.

### Core
| # | Candidate capability | Need served | Drivers | Likely involved entities | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Prepare a serving of hot coffee on demand | a cup when wanted, without preparing more than needed | C | coffee drinker, coffee-making equipment | S: guide's single-cup routine |
| 2 | Tailor the serving to the occasion (amount and strength) | different cups for different moods and vessels | C | coffee drinker | S: four amounts, a strength option |
| 3 | Obtain ready-to-use coffee supplies in variety | coffee without measuring or handling loose ingredients | C, M | supplier of coffee supplies, retailer or delivery, coffee drinker | S: variety statement; I: ready-to-use form removes measuring and handling |
| 4 | Dispose of used coffee remains and packaging without mess | clean and quick clean-up after a cup | M | coffee drinker, household waste handling | S: used item removed after brewing; I: remains stay contained (confirm in the teardown source) |
| 5 | Contain and clear spills and drips | a clean counter, any vessel | M | coffee drinker, vessel | S: overflow is captured and emptied occasionally; travel-vessel placement described |

### Enabling

### Implementation details held for later phases
Evidence from OBS-0006 that names the design; it will be used in SA, LA and PA, not in the OA wording.
- **Pod and its holder:** the sealed single-serve pod, pod holder assembly, funnel and the two needles (support capabilities 1, 3, 4 and 10).
- **Drip tray, tray plate and base:** support capability 5 and vessel placement.
- **Water reservoir, MAX line, lock tabs, optional filter:** support capability 7.
- **Brew size and strength buttons, indicator light, auto power-down, altitude setting:** support capabilities 2, 8 and 9.
- **Descaling procedure and needle-cleaning method:** support capability 10.

### Candidates that may move or be dropped when tuning
- **8 and 9** may read better as System Analysis functions or scenarios.
- **11** may be a cross-cutting constraint, not a capability.
- **10 and 12** may be one lifecycle capability or stay split; splitting keeps cleaning (which supports "lack of mess") visible.
- **3 and 4** are where the design's value will show most once SA to PA are modeled, since they express the supply and clean-up needs that a portioned, contained format answers. They should carry the comparison with traditional preparation (DEC-0005).

### Questions for tuning
1. **"A serving" in capability 1:** is "serving" a need (an amount for one person), or should the OA stay silent on amount and leave it to capability 2?
2. **Granularity:** about a dozen capabilities, or fewer higher-level ones with the detail in activities and scenarios?
3. **Entities:** is "coffee-making equipment" an operational entity in OA, or does equipment stay outside until System Analysis? (With DEC-0008, a generic entity is possible; a product-specific one is not.)
4. **Baseline:** add the traditional preparation process now as a comparison, to quantify the convenience and mess indicators (DEC-0005)?
5. **Suppliers:** one generic supplier of coffee supplies, or several?

### Next steps
Tune the list with the engineer; then derive operational entities, activities and scenarios; then add to the model through `capella-fabric` with a small first patch (this will also be Compat finding 2).
