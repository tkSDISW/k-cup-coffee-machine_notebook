## DRAFT: goal (reach) requirements: sustainability and waste by-products

**Status: working draft for tuning; nothing is in the model yet.** Companion to the base user requirements entry. These are goals that a design aims to approach, expected to be key drivers for future designs. They are written so any design, current or future, can be assessed on the same terms; **they are not statements about any current product.** Solution-agnostic (DEC-0008). Evidence tags: **U** stated by the user, **S** sourced, **I** inferred (to confirm), **A** assumed. Values are **TBD** unless a source gives them.

**Measurement idea:** express each goal per serving of coffee, so designs of different formats compare fairly, and against a stated baseline (for example traditional preparation of an equivalent serving, defined in the OA comparison from DEC-0005).

### Waste and by-products
| ID | Goal | Rationale | Capability | Verification | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| UR-G-01 | **Solid waste per serving** (used coffee remains plus all discarded packaging) shall not exceed **TBD** grams, measured as the mass of material discarded per serving. | The waste a drinker generates each cup is a leading operational sustainability indicator. | 4 | Weigh discarded material over a defined number of servings. | U (waste is a key driver); value TBD |
| UR-G-02 | Packaging for coffee supplies shall be **recyclable or compostable** through widely available routes, for at least **TBD** percent of its mass, with clear marking for the drinker. | Packaging is a large share of the by-products in some formats; end-of-life routes decide whether it is diverted from landfill. | 3, 4 | Material declaration plus check against a recognized recyclability or compostability criterion (to select). | U; criteria to select |
| UR-G-03 | Used coffee remains shall be **divertible to a beneficial use** (for example composting) with no more than **TBD** additional drinker steps and no loose mess. | Used grounds are organic material that can have a second use; the effort to separate them affects both mess and diversion. | 4 | Count of steps and observation of mess in a timed handling test. | I from DEC-0005 and U |
| UR-G-04 | **Packaging mass per serving** shall not exceed **TBD** grams. | Reducing packaging at the source reduces waste before recycling is needed. | 3 | Weigh packaging per serving. | U; value TBD |

### Candidate frameworks to select from (not verified in this session)
Life cycle assessment methods and standards, and standards or schemes for recyclability and compostability claims. Evidence: **A**; to be searched and cited when we pick.

### Questions for tuning
1. **Baseline:** is traditional preparation of an equivalent serving the comparison for UR-G-01 to UR-G-08, or a defined reference design?
2. **Ordering:** which goals are most important to you? I would lead with UR-G-01 to UR-G-04 (waste and by-products), then energy and water.
3. **Boundary:** do we count the coffee supply chain (growing, roasting, shipping) in the life cycle goal, or only the use phase and packaging?
4. **Values:** do you want to set numeric targets now, or keep them TBD until we have a baseline measured from the operational comparison?

Wording is descriptive and neutral. These are goals for evaluating any design on equal terms.
