## DRAFT (revision 2): goal (reach) requirements: sustainability and waste by-products

**Status: working draft for tuning; nothing is in the model yet.** Revision 2 applies DEC-0009. Companion to the base user requirements (NOTE-0003). These are goals that a design aims to approach, expected to be key drivers for future designs. They are written so any design, current or future, can be assessed on the same terms; **they are not statements about any current product.** Solution-agnostic (DEC-0008). Evidence tags: **U** stated by the user, **S** sourced, **I** inferred (to confirm), **A** assumed. Values are **TBD** unless a source gives them.

**Scope of the problem space (DEC-0009):** the **use phase and the packaging of coffee supplies**. The coffee supply chain (growing, roasting, shipping) is out of scope. As understood, manufacturing of the equipment itself is also outside the boundary for now (to confirm).

**Waste baseline: "coffee in, coffee out" (DEC-0009).** The minimum possible waste per serving is the **used coffee remains** themselves. **Everything else that is discarded, in particular packaging, counts as waste.** Waste goals therefore measure what is discarded beyond the used coffee remains, per serving. *Interpretation to confirm.*

**Measurement idea:** express each goal per serving of coffee, so designs of different formats compare fairly.

### Questions still open

2. **Equipment boundary:** is the equipment's own manufacturing outside the boundary, and does its end-of-life (UR-G-09) stay as a goal?
3. **Ordering:** which goals matter most? I would lead with UR-G-01 to UR-G-04 (waste and by-products), then energy and water.
4. **Values:** do you want numeric targets now, or keep them TBD until a baseline is measured from the operational comparison?

Wording is descriptive and neutral. These are goals for evaluating any design on equal terms.
