### Observations from the mapping (neutral, for tuning)
1. **Capability 5 (contain and clear spills and drips) has no requirement.** The mess thesis (DEC-0005) is covered for used remains and packaging by UR-G-01 to UR-G-04, but nothing yet states a need for spills and drips. A requirement could be added (an opportunity, for example a limit on spills or drips per serving, with the measurement defined).
2. **Capabilities 9 and 11 have no requirement.** Both were already flagged in NOTE-0002: 9 may move to System Analysis, and 11 may become a cross-cutting constraint rather than a capability. A safety requirement may be needed regardless.
3. **Every requirement from UR-B-01 to UR-G-09 has at least one capability.** The three stretch requirements (UR-G-10 to UR-G-12) have none listed because they extend a base requirement.
4. **UR-G-08 as drafted points at every capability,** which makes 12 links. It is an assessment requirement over use and packaging; it can be linked to all, to a subset, or kept as a cross-cutting requirement without capability links.

### Decisions needed before or while drawing
1. **UR-G-08:** link to all 12 capabilities, to a subset (for example the serving, supplies and disposal capabilities that carry the use-phase and packaging impact), or to none?
2. **UR-G-10 to UR-G-12:** link each to the capabilities of the base requirement it stretches, or leave them unlinked and rely on their "stretches" note?
3. **Gaps:** add a spill and drip requirement for capability 5, and a safety requirement for capability 11?

### After drawing
The engineer pushes from Capella; the agent pulls, reads back what the tools can show (relation count and type on each requirement, and that the model verifies clean), and updates the Status column. The read-back cannot show direction reliably (Compat finding 9), so direction stays a check in Capella.

Wording is descriptive and neutral; this table is a working checklist, not a statement about any product.
