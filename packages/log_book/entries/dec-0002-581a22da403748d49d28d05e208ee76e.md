## Decision: source set and working practices for the K-Cup model

**Sources selected** (from the survey in obs-0001): the SmartKeurig hacking repos, the Ohio State Keurig teardown, and the iFixit K-Duo page, with Wikipedia for operational context.

**Expected source strength by ARCADIA phase** (engineer's assessment, adopted as planning assumption)
- **Operational Analysis (OA) and System Analysis (SA):** open sources are weak. **User manuals** (use-and-care manuals) are the expected best fit for stakeholders, operational scenarios, user-facing capabilities and functional exchanges. Still to locate and log.
- **Logical Architecture (LA) and Physical Architecture (PA):** the teardowns are the strong fit (component structure, interfaces, sensors, actuators, materials).

**Process:** follow the ARCADIA method, plus the extra practices from the `Capella Modeling Practices` notebook (`tkSDISW/Capella_Modeling_Practices`, read-only scope, agent has read it in full). Practices adopted for this project:
1. **Capture reference resources in the log book before modeling begins**: exact model/part numbers, key specs, and where each came from (`modeling_with_cartenza_support`).
2. **PMEI prefix on Component Exchange names** (P physical, M material, E energy, I information); Functional Exchanges keep names for what they carry. P is expected to be rare or absent at LA and to appear at PA; split an exchange that carries more than one category (`naming_conventions`).
3. **Essence over transition default for LA decomposition**: decompose logical functions first, replace coarse transitioned LA functions with domain-grounded ones, rebuild SA→LA realization links by hand, then review the mapping as a table or diagram (`la_decomposition_methodology`).

**Rationale:** these practices came from a prior project (the Residential AC logbook) and were generalized; reusing them avoids rediscovering the same lessons and keeps the model consistent and reviewable.
