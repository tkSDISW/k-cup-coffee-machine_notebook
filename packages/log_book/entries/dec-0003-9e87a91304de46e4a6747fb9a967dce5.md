## Decision: reference sources are the Ohio State B130 teardown and the iFixit K-Duo page

**Decided by the engineer after reviewing the candidate links.**

**Selected**
- **Ohio State teardown (Keurig B130):** component structure, sensing, actuation, materials and pod interface, mainly for LA and PA. The original is the Fictiv B130 teardown, to be read directly.
- **iFixit page (Keurig K-Duo, 2019):** published specs, replacement guides, and links to the **K-Duo use and care manual**, mainly for OA and SA, with the guides also informing LA and PA.

**Not selected for now:** the SmartKeurig repos (Keurig K40). They remain listed in OBS-0002 as an optional cross-check only.

**Consequences**
- The use and care manual replaces the earlier plan to hunt for a K40 manual. The **K-Duo manual is the OA/SA source to read next**.
- OBS-0002 is the reference-specifications record for these sources.

**Open issue: the two selected sources still describe two different models**
- The **B130** is a pod-only brewer. The **K-Duo** is a hybrid (pod plus carafe with hot plate).
- The model needs a stated scope and a stated relationship between the sources. Options to decide:
  1. **K-Duo as the basis, pod-brewing mode only.** Carafe, hot plate and auto-brew are out of scope or deferred. B130 teardown is used as a structural analogue for the pod brewing path (needle puncture, heater, pumps, lid switch).
  2. **K-Duo as the basis, full scope.** Carafe path and hot plate are modeled too, with B130 informing only the shared pod path.
  3. **A generic "single-serve pod brewer"** with the two products as evidence, which makes the model product-agnostic but not traceable to one nameplate.
- Whichever is chosen, **every B130-derived element used on a K-Duo model must be logged as an assumption**, since the two products are not the same machine.

**Next steps:** read the K-Duo manual and the Fictiv teardown; settle the scope option above; then confirm `purpose` and `behavior_config` and begin Operational Analysis.
