## DRAFT: operational activities, exchange items and interactions for the operational processes (for alignment before patching)

**Status: working draft; nothing is in the model yet.** Division of work agreed with the engineer: **we align on the activities and exchanges allocated to the entities; the agent patches them in; the engineer creates the operational processes in Capella and maps them to the operational capabilities.** Wording is solution-agnostic (DEC-0008), neutral (behavior config), and written so that exchanges are named for what they carry (the naming practice). Evidence tags: **S** sourced (K-Duo guide, OBS in `log_book`), **I** inferred, **A** assumed.

### What the patch tool can and cannot create here
- **Can (per the tool's current description):** operational activities, allocation of activities to entities (to confirm by test), exchange items in the OA data package, and **operational interactions between activities** through the `exchange:` form, which fills in the owning activity itself.
- **Handbook mismatch to resolve:** an older Handbook note says exchanges cannot be created by patch, because they connect ports. The tool's own description now says OA interactions can. I will test with a small patch first and log the outcome as a compatibility finding. Entity-level communication means are not in scope for this tool.
- **Not by patch, as before:** component exchanges and physical links; custom cardinality on exchange items (they default to 1).
- **The processes are yours:** I do not create operational processes; you build them from the activities and interactions below.

**Capabilities without a process here:** *Operate across a range of conditions* and *Operate safely around heat, electricity and sharp parts*. Both were already flagged as possible System Analysis items or cross-cutting constraints.

### How requirements could attach to this structure (for the paper review, DEC-0010)
- UR-B-02 (serving temperature) and UR-B-03 (strength): properties of the exchange item **Served coffee**.
- UR-B-04 (cycle time): a latency constraint on the process PR1 (a time from the serving request to served coffee), with separate cases for ready, cold-start and consecutive servings.
- UR-G-01 and UR-G-03 (waste and diversion): the item **Used coffee remains and packaging** flowing to Waste Handling in PR3.
- UR-G-04 (packaging mass and ratio): the item **Coffee supplies** in PR2.
- UR-G-05 (energy): the item **Electrical power**; UR-G-06 (water): the item **Water**; UR-G-07 (consumables) and the cleaning water: **Used cleaning liquid** and PR7.

### Questions for tuning
1. **Granularity:** is 28 activities and 30 interactions about right, or fewer, coarser activities (for example merging "Choose and request the serving" with "Receive the serving request" would be a design hint, so it is kept separate)?
2. **Entities with few activities:** are Equipment Support (1), Utilities Supply (2) and Coffee Supply Chain (2) right as small entities?
3. **Used cleaning liquid** and **Mark packaging with disposal guidance** are my additions to carry the waste and packaging goals; keep or drop?
4. **Store or move the equipment** stands alone; leave it so, or give it an exchange?
5. **Two capabilities without a process:** leave them for System Analysis?

Wording is descriptive and neutral; this is a first draft for tuning, not a statement about any product.
