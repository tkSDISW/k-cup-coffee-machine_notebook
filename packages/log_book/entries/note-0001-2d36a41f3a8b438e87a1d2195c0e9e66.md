## purpose: K Cup Coffee Maker Notebook

A working log book for building an open, traceable Capella/ARCADIA model of a K-Cup pod brewer (Keurig K-Duo, pod-brewing mode only). Its centerpiece is an Operational Analysis that explains why single-serve pods succeeded through convenience and lack of mess, and what bar that sets for next-generation products.

The notebook records sources, assumptions, decisions and lessons so the path from operational need to physical design can be followed, reused and reviewed. It aims to go beyond existing open coffee machine examples in depth, realism and traceability.

**Audience:** the engineer building the model, reviewers, and potentially the makers of this product and of future products, with whom it is intended to be shared. It is therefore written to be neutral, descriptive and respectful (see behavior_config).

---

**Educational purpose.** The model and this notebook are built for educational purposes: to demonstrate, with open sources and a real product as the subject, how Operational Analysis through Physical Architecture can be developed, documented and traced in Capella using the ARCADIA method.
