## Decision: the drafts are moved into the model, with this field mapping

**Decided by the engineer** ("move into the model what we have"). The current drafts (NOTE-0002, NOTE-0003 and NOTE-0004, all revision 2) are created in the Capella 6.1 model. They stay drafts: values remain TBD, and the open tuning questions in the notes are not settled by this move.

**What is now in the model** (local commits; see "Push status")
- **16 requirements,** 4 in "Base User Requirements" and 12 in "Reach Requirements" (UR-B-01 to UR-B-04, UR-G-01 to UR-G-12).
- **12 operational capabilities** in the Operational Capabilities package (the 12 candidates of NOTE-0002 revision 2, named as in the draft).
- Still **no operational entities, activities or scenarios.**

**Field mapping for requirements** (settles open item 3 of DEC-0011)
| Draft field | Capella field |
|---|---|
| Requirement ID (UR-B-nn, UR-G-nn) | `name` |
| Short title | `long_name` |
| The "shall" statement, with TBD values kept as TBD | `text` |
| Rationale, capability numbers, verification method, evidence tag and source draft | `description` (plain sentences) |

- **Capability numbers in the description** are references to NOTE-0002 numbering, not model links. Traceability links between requirements and capabilities **are not yet created**; the paper's justification and satisfaction links remain to be made (DEC-0010).
- **Verification categories** (inspection, analysis, demonstration, test) were **proposed by the agent** as part of this move, to close the gap noted in DEC-0010. Each is marked "proposed category" in the description for the engineer to confirm or change.
- **Base versus goal values:** base requirements and goal requirements are separate requirements in separate modules, as drafted. Representing a threshold and a goal on the same element is still an open question (DEC-0010).

**Capabilities:** the name is the capability wording of the draft; the description holds the need served, the drivers, the draft source, and notes on capabilities that may move or combine when tuned.

**Commits (local, not pushed unless stated):** `0afe0269` (UR-B-04, pushed earlier), `74a894bd` (15 requirements), `c0ad724e` (UR-B-04 description), `c742d25b` (12 capabilities).

**Push status:** the three newest commits await the engineer's confirmation.

**Open items**
1. The engineer's check in Capella 6.1 after push (see Compat finding 4).
2. Create the traceability links between requirements and capabilities, in Capella or by a method to be tested.
3. Operational entities, activities and scenarios, which need answers to the tuning questions in NOTE-0002 (granularity, whether equipment is an entity, baseline, suppliers).
4. Update the three draft notes to point to the model, or keep them as the editable source until the engineer's review is finished (to decide).
