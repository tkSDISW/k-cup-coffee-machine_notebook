## K-Duo Use and Care Guide read in full; variant to be pinned down

Reference capture for the OA/SA source named in DEC-0003. Paraphrased; the manual is a copyrighted vendor document, so check terms before reusing figures or text.

**Document read:** [Use & Care Guide, K-Duo Coffee Maker (official PDF)](https://dam.keurig.com/content/dam/public-assets/kcom_support/10272_K-Duo_U%26CG_Digital_RevC.pdf). Printed document number DC0000002753 REV C; file name carries 10272 and RevC. The same document is mirrored by a university site, and a copy is linked from the iFixit page (that copy was not re-read). Quick start guides were not read.

**Issue: the K-Duo family has several variants.** Search results show separate guides for K-Duo, K-Duo Plus, K-Duo Essentials, K-Duo Essentials Gen 2 and K-Duo Special Edition. The iFixit page is titled K-Duo (2019) and links a guide titled "Essentials". The guide read here lists Smart Start, Auto Brew, Strong, High Altitude and Pause and Pour. **It is not confirmed that this guide, the iFixit specs and the physical product we model are the same variant.** Open item: choose the exact variant and confirm that the specs in OBS-0002 (1470 W, 60 oz reservoir, 170-190 °F, and so on) belong to it.

**Pod-mode facts used for OA and SA** (all *sourced* from this guide unless marked)
- **First-use setup:** remove packing tape, plug in, place a mug on the drip tray, remove and fill the water reservoir to the MAX line, replace it with its lock tabs engaged, power on, set the clock (needed only for Auto Brew), then run a cleansing brew without a pod. Initial heating takes about 4 minutes; reheating between brews about 1 minute.
- **Single-cup brew:** lift the handle, place a pod in the pod holder, lower the handle fully, power on, select POD, select a brew size (buttons 6, 8, 10, 12), optionally select STRONG, press BREW. Brewing ends when the brew indicator light goes out.
- **Vessel:** a large mug (12 oz minimum) goes on the drip tray; for a travel mug, remove the drip tray and place the mug on the drip tray base.
- **Used pod:** the guide says to remove the used pod from the pod holder after brewing, or as part of the daily cleaning routine.
- **Spill containment:** the drip tray holds up to 8 oz of overflow and is emptied and cleaned occasionally.
- **Energy:** after the last single-cup brew, the brewer enters Auto Off after 5 minutes; power button reactivates it.
- **Altitude:** at about 5,000 feet, a High Altitude setting is enabled by holding the POD and CARAFE buttons for 3 seconds; a manual puncture step is also described.
- **Care:** wipe exterior; clean reservoir and lid periodically; clean the pod holder, funnel, exit needle and entrance needle (paper-clip method, then two water-only brews); run a cleansing brew or rinse pod after sweetened pods; **descale every 3 months** or when volume drops, about 75 minutes using descaling solution; parts are not dishwasher safe.
- **Safety:** sharp needles above and below the pod; the guide cautions against putting fingers in the pod holder.
- **Troubleshooting themes:** grounds in the cup, no brew (handle not fully down, add-water light, buttons blinking together), partial cup, no power or shut-off.
- **Storage and transport:** empty the reservoir, store upright and frost-free, power off, allow warm-up if it was below freezing, and rinse before reuse.
- **Water quality:** the guide recommends bottled or filtered water, and describes an optional water filter; it advises against distilled water.
- **Support and parts:** replacement parts through the manufacturer's site; beyond the described cleaning, the brewer is described as not user-serviceable; warranty service by phone.
- **Pod ecosystem (marketing line):** the guide says there are over 70 brands and hundreds of varieties of pods. The warranty text says the manufacturer's own pods and accessories are what guarantee proper function. *Source statements, recorded without adoption.*

**Pod-relevant parts listed:** water reservoir lid, water reservoir, MAX fill line, reservoir lock tabs, power cord, handle, pod holder and funnel (together the pod holder assembly), pod assembly housing, drip tray plate, drip tray, drip tray base. Carafe-side parts (carafe and lid, heating plate, filter basket and cover) are out of scope per DEC-0004.

**Out of scope per DEC-0004 (present in the guide):** carafe brewing, Auto Brew, Pause and Pour, heating plate, paper filter and ground coffee handling.
